# Adhkar sources and how to change the data

The Adhkar tab (`lib/features/adhkar`) is driven by files in `assets/adhkar/`.
Adding or fixing content never needs a code change.

## Files

| File | What it is | SHA-256 |
|---|---|---|
| `manifest.json` | Lists the collections: id, titles (ar/en), group, icon, source file, checksum, which entries to take, optional reminder time and prayer window | not checked (it holds the checksums) |
| `morning_evening.ar.json` | `ar.json` of [Seen-Arabic/Morning-And-Evening-Adhkar-DB](https://github.com/Seen-Arabic/Morning-And-Evening-Adhkar-DB) at commit `29d7623`, **unmodified** | `9e0dd6a1…c8c21` |
| `LICENSE.seen-arabic.txt` | Its MIT licence (shown in the app's licence page) | `a72f0268…ab0825` |
| `curated.ar.json` | Sleep, waking, after prayer. Generated by `tool/build_adhkar.py` from `tool/adhkar_spec.json`. Never edit by hand | `f6606ee2…cca7d` |
| `hisn.ar.json` | The other Hisn al-Muslim chapters (92 duas in 57 chapters). Generated by `tool/build_hisn.py`. Never edit by hand | `61ebc007…` |
| `hisn_manifest.json` | The collections of those chapters, included by `manifest.json` | not checked |
| `manifest.json` | Groups, the hand-kept collections, and `includes` | not checked |

The loader refuses any file whose SHA-256 differs from the manifest, and a test
fails when a dhikr has no source, a repeat count below one, or an ayah that does not exist.

## Where the evidence comes from

- **Morning and evening (26 and 24 items):** the dataset's own `source`, `fadl`, `count` and
  `hadith_text` fields (hadith reference with the Albani grading).
- **Sleep, waking, after prayer (`curated.ar.json`):** built by `tool/build_adhkar.py` from the
  Arabic editions of [fawazahmed0/hadith-api](https://github.com/fawazahmed0/hadith-api)
  (The Unlicense), pinned to commit `df57907` with a SHA-256 per edition (in the spec).
  - A dhikr taken from a hadith is cut out of **that hadith's own text**, with its tashkeel,
    between two anchor phrases. Nothing religious is typed in the tool.
  - A Quran dhikr stores only surah and ayah numbers. The app draws the words from the
    verified Tanzil text, adds the isti'adha once at the start of a list (An-Nahl 16:98) and the
    basmala before each surah opening, except Al-Fatiha and At-Tawba.
  - The evidence line (collection, number, Albani grading) is built from the edition's data.
    A missing hadith number, a missing phrase, or a weak grading stops the build.
  - Muslim is cited by its printed number (`arabicnumber`); the other books by `hadithnumber`.
    Bukhari and Muslim carry no grading in the data, because every hadith in them is sahih.

## The other Hisn al-Muslim chapters

`tool/build_hisn.py` builds `hisn.ar.json` and `hisn_manifest.json`.

- **Index only.** The list of chapters, their order, and the repeat counts come from the structure of
  Hisn al-Muslim as laid out in [rn0x/Adhkar-json](https://github.com/rn0x/Adhkar-json) (commit `93dc3fc`,
  SHA-256 recorded in `tool/hisn_config.json`; no licence is declared there). Its dua text is used only as a
  search key and is **not** copied: the words shown come from the hadith.
- **A dua is kept only if** its words are found, letter for letter (similarity of at least 97%), in a hadith of
  Bukhari, Muslim, Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi, Nasa'i, or Ibn Majah, and they make up at least 85% of the entry (a
  sentence that merely describes the dua does not count). A hadith outside Bukhari and Muslim must carry a
  sahih or hasan grading by Al-Albani in the data. The words are then cut out of the hadith with its tashkeel,
  and the evidence line is built from the data. Sentences that open like a narration or a ruling ("من قال…",
  "إذا…") are dropped.
- **What was left out:** 129 entries, listed by the tool on every build. Most are duas whose hadith is in Ahmad,
  Ibn Hibban, al-Hakim, al-Tabarani, or Ibn al-Sunni (not in the open editions), duas worded differently in
  the six books, and one-word duas that match too many places. About a third of the book's entries, then, is
  not yet in the app. Nothing without checkable evidence is shown.
- **Hand-checked additions** (`manual` in `tool/hisn_config.json`): "غفرانك", the istikhara dua, "جزاك الله
  خيرا". They go through the same evidence checks.
- **Groups.** Chapters are placed in nine groups (`groups` in `tool/hisn_config.json`, titles in
  `manifest.json`). This grouping is editorial.
- Numbering follows the editions in hadith-api (Sunnah.com numbering for Bukhari, for example 1166 for the
  istikhara hadith).
- Rebuild: `python3 tool/build_hisn.py --cache <folder with the editions> --hisn <adhkar.json>`; check with `--check`.

## How a list is read

- Tapping a list opens it as **steps in a bottom sheet** (`AdhkarSteps`): one dhikr at a time, a counter, and previous
  and next. When a dhikr's count is done the next step comes by itself; when the last is done the sheet says so and offers
  the next list. "Show as a list" opens the whole list as a page.
- A tapped notification opens the same sheet over the Adhkar tab.
- **Layout of Quran text:** the isti'adha and the basmala each sit on their own line, with the ayahs between ﴿ ﴾ below
  (`layoutAdhkarText`, for the dataset's text, and `composePassage`, for passages). Only line breaks are added.
- **"After Maghrib only":** an entry may carry `"when": ["maghrib"]` (any of `fajr`, `dhuhr`, `asr`, `maghrib`, `isha`). The
  card shows "After Maghrib only". In `tool/adhkar_spec.json` the same field is written on the entry. The first such entry
  is "لا إله إلا الله وحده… ×10" after Maghrib (Tirmidhi 3534, Albani: hasan sahih). The Fajr version of that dhikr (Tirmidhi
  3474) and "اللهم أجرني من النار ×7" (Abu Dawud 5079) are graded weak by Al-Albani in the data, so they are not included.
- `python3 tool/build_adhkar.py` writes the new checksum into `manifest.json` itself.

## Search, favourites, and groups

- Search folds tashkeel, the forms of alef and ya, and ta marbuta, so a name or words typed without tashkeel
  find the dhikr. List names come first (earlier matches first), then the adhkar whose words hold every word
  typed. The words of Quran dhikr are searched once the Quran text has loaded.
- A star on a list adds it to the favourites (stored on the device), shown under "suggested now".

## Entries that are not fully machine-checked

Listed by the tool as `note:` on every build. Have a scholar review these before a public release.

| Entry | What is checked | What is not |
|---|---|---|
| Istighfar ×3 after prayer | Muslim 591 says he asked forgiveness three times | The exact words "أستغفر الله" are the standard form; the narration does not quote them |
| Tasbih, tahmid, takbir ×33 (after prayer, before sleep) | The cited hadith contains the counts | The one-line wording is the standard form; the hadith quotes the counts, not the words |
| Ayat al-Kursi after prayer | The ayah is verified against Tanzil | The hadith (Nasa'i, *Amal al-Yawm wa al-Layla*) is not in hadith-api, so its reference line is typed, without a number |

## Open items

- **Hisn al-Muslim text rights.** The MIT licence covers Seen-Arabic's work. The underlying book
  (Sa'id al-Qahtani, d. 2018) has several publishers and I found no statement that its text may be
  redistributed. Ask the publisher or IslamHouse before release.
- More collections (the rest of Hisn al-Muslim, duas) follow the same path: add entries to the spec, run
  the tool, add a manifest block. A source with no per-dhikr evidence is not used.
- English translations are not bundled; the interface is localized, the adhkar are Arabic.

## How to change the data

1. **Fix or add a curated dhikr:** edit `tool/adhkar_spec.json`, then
   `python3 tool/build_adhkar.py --cache <empty folder>` (downloads the editions once). It prints the
   new SHA-256.
2. **Update the checksum** in `assets/adhkar/manifest.json` for every collection that uses that file
   (`shasum -a 256 assets/adhkar/curated.ar.json`).
3. **Add a collection:** a block in the manifest. `variants` picks entries by their `type` number;
   `group` is `daily`, `prayer` or `duas`; `sessionWindowMinutes` makes counts last that long after the
   last tap (the after-prayer list uses 30); `reminderTime` (`HH:mm`) gives it a reminder.
4. Run `fvm flutter test test/features/adhkar`.

Check that the generated file is current: `python3 tool/build_adhkar.py --cache <folder> --check`.

## Reminders

Daily local notifications through `flutter_local_notifications`, inexact alarms (no exact-alarm
permission, which Google Play restricts). The permission is requested only when the person turns a
reminder on. Nothing is sent off the device.

**Prayer times and alerts** live in `lib/features/prayer` (`adhan`, MIT): times are worked out on the device from
an approximate location (rounded to about a kilometre, read once while the app is open, kept only on the device).
The calculation method follows the place (a coarse map of regions in `PrayerMethod.forPlace`: Egyptian for Egypt,
Sudan, Libya, the Levant and Iraq; Umm al-Qura for Arabia; Dubai, Kuwait, Qatar, Turkey, Karachi for South Asia,
Singapore for the Malay world, ISNA for North America; Muslim World League elsewhere) until the person chooses one.
Two kinds of notification are scheduled from it: an alert at each prayer the person picks, and the after-prayer
adhkar reminder. The next seven days are scheduled as one-time notifications and scheduled again each time the
app opens, so opening the app at least once a week keeps them going. Starting the app never asks for a
permission. See `docs/STORE_COMPLIANCE.md`.
